Religious Communities Under Pressure

Documenting Religious Persecution in Russia 2022–2026
Author: Sergei V. Chapnin
Foreword: Dr. Rowan Williams
Published: June 13, 2026
Pages: 163
ISBN: 979-8950169038
Language: English

This report documents the systematic persecution of religious communities in Russia during and after the full-scale war against Ukraine. Based on interviews, archival research, and documentary evidence, Sergei Chapnin examines the state's suppression of independent religious communities, the Russian Orthodox Church's complicity in state violence, and the theological and ethical questions these developments raise for believers and scholars worldwide.

The book includes analysis of legal persecution, theological corruption, and the weaponization of religious language and imagery by state and church authorities. It is essential reading for scholars of religion and politics, human rights advocates, and anyone seeking to understand the religious dimensions of contemporary Russian authoritarianism.
